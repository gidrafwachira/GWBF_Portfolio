Who I am

I am a programme and grant management leader with 12+ years leading multi-country climate, food systems and agri-tech programmes across Africa and Asia. Most recently, as Global Climate Intersections Lead at NetHope, I led its climate adaptation and resilience programme in seven countries. I ran a $1.5M+ annual climate grant portfolio that deployed more than $3M to 25 organisations (majority CBOs) through flexible, locally led grantmaking. I also led capacity building initiatives for community-based organisations and local leaders in community-led climate action and advocacy through multi-day trainings, self-paced online courses and peer learning forums. Our community early warning grants, community-based adaptation trainings, community voices amplification and digital skilling programmes raised adaptive capacity for over 211,000 people by up to 40%.

Before that, I led Vodacom's Connected Farmer programme across Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania from pilot to scale. It reached 250,000+ smallholder farmers with a 73–122% income uplift through climate-smart practices, insurance and finance, and I supported 15+ community organisations with grants and technical assistance. Earlier, I launched Agri-wallet in Rwanda from zero, reaching 40,000 farmers.

Why GWBF, and why I fit the Regional Program Lead, East Africa role

  • Shared values. Communities hold the solutions to their own challenges, so they should set the priorities. Funders and partners come in to complement, with flexible, multi-year support.
  • The work I do best. Walking alongside communities to build their climate and economic resilience: up to 40% higher adaptive capacity for 211,000+ community members across 7 countries, and economic resilience for 250,000+ farmers in East Africa.
  • Displacement and climate together. In Yumbe District, Uganda, I worked with host and refugee communities around Bidibidi settlement, where climate stress, scarce resources and gender-based violence are linked.
  • My region. Nairobi-based, with programme experience across Kenya and Uganda and regular regional travel.
